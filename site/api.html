PUBLIC DATA

Read the
raw data.

Everything the Shipwatch site shows comes from four public JSON addresses. They need no key and no account. The observer checks each watched site every five minutes, so the feed is updated every five minutes. Field names and shapes may change as Shipwatch grows, so read them defensively.

01 · FEED

Watched projects and what changed

Address
https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/api/feed
Top level
observed_at, interval_seconds, targets[], events[]
events[]
id, mint, observed, previous_observation, kind, summary, added[], removed[], before_hash, after_hash, detail_url, repeat_count, association_ambiguous, symbol, name, url
kind
baseline, page, assets, release, repository, unavailable, recovered
targets[]
mint, symbol, name, url, link_source, added, checked, status, title, error, release_info, latest_signal, assets_seen, assets_read
Times
ISO 8601, in UTC.
curl -s https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/api/feed

Names, symbols, titles, excerpts and errors come from the watched websites and their listings, written by people we do not know. Treat them as untrusted text. A recurring change is listed once, as its latest occurrence.

02 · STATS

Visit totals

Address
https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/api/stats
Fields
counting_since, visitors, opens, projects_watched, days[]
days[]
day, visitors, opens, for up to the last 30 days, newest first.
curl -s https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/api/stats

Daily totals only. No cookie and no stored identifier are behind them.

03 · HEALTH

Is the observer running

Address
https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/health
Fields
project, status, code_sha256
curl -s https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/health

code_sha256 is the hash the server reports for its own source. The verify page compares it with the published build.

04 · POSTS

Posts published on X

Address
https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/posts/receipts.json
Contents
Each post Shipwatch has published on X, with its link.
curl -s https://api.shipwatch.bwltd.kr/posts/receipts.json
