Reflex Works · who we are

Small tools,
built in public.

Reflex Works is an independent studio. We build small, useful tools for people who trade and build on Solana, and we publish all of it: the source, the build, and the way to check both.

01 · How we work

Five rules
we ship by.

  1. Open from the first release.

    The source of everything we serve is public. The site you use is built from that source by a public build you can read.

  2. Checkable, not “trust us”.

    Each product has a page that hashes the files in your own browser and compares them with the repository. If they differ, it says so.

  3. Small releases, said plainly.

    We ship often and in small steps, and each release says what a visitor can now do. Nothing is announced before it is live.

  4. No tricks around a token.

    When a product has a token, the wallet that creates it is named before the launch, the team's purchase and its sell policy are published, and we never promise a price.

  5. No trackers.

    No third-party scripts and no cookies. Visits are counted as daily totals, and the totals are public.
